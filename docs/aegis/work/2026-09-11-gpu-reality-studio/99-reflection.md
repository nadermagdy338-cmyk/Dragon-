# Reflection

Three review rounds steadily moved the design from "two vendor pages with duplicated writers" to one proof-driven control plane. The deepest lesson: capability truth is temporal — a provider must be re-proven at transaction time, its effective state (e.g. an active MediaTek lock) must be re-derived at read time, and persisted intents must re-resolve against the hardware present at restore time rather than replaying stale values. The second round's finding that ambiguity must block even governor writes is the strictest reading of "diagnostics-only", and it is the right one. Remaining risk is concentrated in unexecuted bytecode: with no JVM on this host, the unit suite is authored-but-unrun, and real-driver behavior (MTK proc parsing especially) needs on-device validation.
